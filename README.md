# portfolio-legal-web-designer

## Purpose

This project showcases a unique specialization at the intersection of **Law and Web Design**.  
The portfolio demonstrates how legal expertise can be embedded directly into UX/UI and WordPress development, creating websites that are not only visually appealing but also **compliant, accessible, and risk‑reducing**.

---

## Why This Matters

Most designers don’t understand law.  
Most lawyers don’t understand UX.  
My profile bridges that gap.

I help businesses:
- Reduce compliance risks (GDPR, accessibility, IP rights)
- Build trust with users through transparent design
- Transform complex legal obligations into clear user experiences

---

## Services Offered

- **Legally Compliant Web Design**  
  WordPress sites designed with GDPR integration, legal mentions, and accessibility by default.

- **Legal & UX Audits**  
  Identify non‑compliance, dark patterns, and UX risks. Provide actionable redesigns and mockups.

- **Legal Design for Documents & Interfaces**  
  Visual CGU/CGV, readable privacy policies, and UX‑friendly consent flows.

- **Accessibility & Compliance Consulting**  
  Diagnostics, inclusive design, and regulatory alignment (WCAG, RGAA).

---

## Competitive Edge

I don’t sell design.

I sell **legal clarity + UX security**.  

This positioning is rare, durable, and highly valuable for SMEs, startups, and law firms.
