# Legal & Structural Requirements

## Purpose

This document outlines the legal and structural requirements applied to the
**Legal Web Designer Portfolio**.

The objective is to ensure that the website is compliant by design, transparent
for users, and aligned with best practices in legal UX.

---

## Core Site Structure

The portfolio includes the following core pages:

### Content Pages

- **Home**: Value proposition, positioning, and main call-to-action.
- **Services**: Clear presentation of professional offerings.
- **Projects**: Case studies demonstrating legal and UX problem-solving.
- **About**: Professional background and methodology.
- **Contact**: Secure and minimal contact form.

### Legal Pages

- **Legal Notice (Imprint)**  
  Provides identification of the website owner and legal responsibility.

- **Privacy Policy**  
  Explains how personal data is collected, processed, and protected.

- **Accessibility Statement**  
  Declares the level of accessibility, standards followed (WCAG),
  and known limitations.

---

## Legal Principles Applied

### 1. Transparency

Users must easily understand:

- Who operates the website
- What services are offered
- How their data is handled

### 2. Data Protection by Design

- Minimal data collection on contact forms
- Clear purpose limitation
- No pre-checked consent boxes
- Explicit and informed consent where required

### 3. Accessibility as a Legal Requirement

- Semantic HTML structure
- Readable content hierarchy
- Commitment to WCAG 2.1 AA standards

### 4. UX Without Dark Patterns

- No misleading buttons or forced consent
- Clear navigation and readable legal content
- Equal visibility of acceptance and refusal options where applicable

---

## Visibility & Navigation Requirements

- Legal pages must be accessible from the footer.
- Privacy-related information must be easy to locate.
- Accessibility information must be publicly available.

---

## Compliance Scope & Limitations

This portfolio is a professional demonstration website:

- No commercial transactions are performed.
- No sensitive personal data is processed.
- Compliance measures are implemented as best-practice examples.
