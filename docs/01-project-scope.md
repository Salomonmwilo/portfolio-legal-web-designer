# Project Scope: Legal Web Designer Portfolio

## 1. Purpose

This project aims to build a professional WordPress portfolio that demonstrates a rare specialization at the intersection of **Law and Web Design**.  
The portfolio will showcase how legal expertise can be embedded into UX/UI and WordPress development, resulting in websites that are **compliant, accessible, and risk‑reducing**.

---

## 2. Need

Most designers lack legal knowledge, and most lawyers lack UX/UI expertise.  
This portfolio bridges that gap by combining:

- Legal clarity
- UX security
- Compliance by design

**Target outcome:** A strong freelance profile on platforms like Upwork, offering services that reassure clients about legal compliance and user experience.

---

## 3. Solution

**Positioning:** _Legal Design & Compliance by Design_

Key principles:

- **Legal Design** → Making law readable and usable
- **Compliance by Design** → Integrating legal requirements into UX/UI
- **Risk Reduction** → Helping businesses minimize compliance risks

---

## 4. Key Legal Domains

- **Intellectual Property Law**: copyrights, licenses, rights transfer, co‑creation agreements
- **Data Protection (GDPR)**: privacy by design, consent flows, cookie management, dark pattern avoidance
- **Digital Accessibility**: WCAG/RGAA compliance, inclusive UX, accessibility audits

---

## 5. Services Offered

- **Legally Compliant Web Design**: WordPress sites with GDPR integration, legal mentions, and accessibility
- **Legal & UX Audits**: Identify non‑compliance, dark patterns, and provide corrective mockups
- **Legal Design (Documents + Interfaces)**: Visual CGU/CGV, readable privacy policies, UX‑friendly onboarding
- **Accessibility & Compliance Consulting**: Diagnostics, inclusive design, regulatory alignment

---

## 6. Competitive Advantage

I don’t sell design.  
I don’t sell law.  
I sell **legal clarity + UX security**.

This positioning is rare, durable, and highly valuable for SMEs, startups, and law firms.

---

## 7. Long‑Term Vision

Within 3–5 years, this portfolio evolves into:

- Digital compliance consulting
- Corporate training in Legal Design / GDPR
- Strategic support for startups and law firms

---

## 8. Priorities for Skill Development

1. **GDPR & Privacy by Design** (highest priority)
2. **Legal Design** (premium positioning)
3. **Digital Accessibility** (huge potential)
4. **Intellectual Property Law applied to web**

---

## 9. Transversal Skills

- Translating legal jargon into client‑friendly language
- Packaging services clearly (audits, redesigns, templates)

---

## 10. Target Audience
This portfolio is designed for:
- SMEs and startups seeking legally compliant websites
- Freelancers and independent professionals who need GDPR‑ready WordPress sites
- Law firms and digital platforms requiring accessible and secure interfaces

---

## 11. Legal Value
The portfolio demonstrates how legal expertise can be translated into design decisions that:
- Reduce compliance risks (GDPR, accessibility, IP rights)
- Build user trust through transparent and fair UX
- Provide businesses with a competitive edge by integrating law into design from the start
